# Xenon

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Shoppers browsing clothing and accessories in Sri Lanka, and administrators maintaining the store. The user selected premium fashion positioning on 25 October 2026.

## Product Purpose

Help shoppers discover collections, understand actual garment options and prices, and confidently place guest Cash on Delivery orders in LKR. Help administrators operate the catalog and publish website content without confusing drafts with live commerce changes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js 16.2.9, React 19, Tailwind 3, Base UI and Supabase remain the architecture. Existing catalog, URLs, cart persistence, authentication, revision history and publishing contracts remain intact. Accounts, reviews, wishlists and online payments are not added by this redesign.

## Brand Commitments

Retain the Xenon name and supplied logo artwork. The user approved a premium white-and-black collection-gallery direction, professional Instrument Sans typography, improved shopping/admin flows and a visual preview before storefront implementation. Natural product-photo colors remain. All file changes must remain inside this project folder.

## Evidence on Hand

`public/logo/` contains the original logos; `public/images/hero.png` is existing campaign imagery. Real catalog and published content are supplied by Supabase. Existing screenshots under `docs/screenshots/` are historical fixture evidence, not production certification. Do not fabricate product facts, endorsements, policy promises or sizing.

## Product Principles

- Make discovery and purchase decisions clear, with accurate prices and variant availability.
- Preserve input and explain recovery after request failures.
- Keep operational changes distinct from draft website publishing.
- Use photography, typography and composition for character, without obstructing shopping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and touch operation, visible focus, associated field errors, reduced motion and responsive reflow are requirements. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA without claiming certification.
